Visa information for Botswana

British citizens visiting Botswana for tourism generally do not need a visa. On arrival at the border or airport, a free entry permit (Visitor's Pass) is typically issued, valid for stays of up to 90 days. Your passport must have sufficient remaining validity. Travellers should also carry a return or onward ticket and, if needed, proof of sufficient funds and accommodation. There are no different entry requirements for British citizens compared with other visa-exempt nationalities. Since entry rules can change on short notice, check current requirements before departure.

Entering Botswana in detail: documents & preparation

Preparing for travel abroad requires particular attention to border documents. The entry authorities of Botswana strictly check at border crossings and airports whether all requirements are met. Airlines can face substantial penalties if they carry passengers without valid documents, which is why checks are carried out very carefully before departure.

Why must the passport remain valid for months after the trip?

The rule that a passport must remain valid (often for 3 to 6 months) beyond the date of your planned return confuses many holidaymakers. The reason is a safeguard used by the destination country: if illness, an accident or legal issues mean that you unexpectedly have to stay longer in Botswana, the authorities can ensure that you still have a valid, internationally recognised travel document for your departure even weeks later.

Important for road trips: driving licence and car hire in Botswana

If you plan to hire a car locally, you should not only keep an eye on your passport validity. Many car hire companies in Botswana check very carefully at the desk whether the identity documents match the details on the driving licence. In many countries outside the EU, an International Driving Permit is also mandatory in addition to your national driving licence. You should also make sure that the credit card for the security deposit is issued in the name of the main driver, otherwise the hire car may be refused despite the booking.

Biometric passport vs temporary passport

For most intercontinental flights, a national ID card is not enough. You need a biometric electronic passport (ePassport) with an integrated chip. If you book at short notice and your passport has expired, you can apply for a fast-track passport or, in an emergency, a temporary passport. Please note: not every country accepts a temporary passport for entry or for applying for an electronic visa (e-visa). You should always check this in advance on the official embassy website.

Travelling with children: note the new rules

Since 1 January 2024, older child passport arrangements are no longer sufficient for many journeys outside the EU and the Schengen Area. Babies and young children generally now need a standard biometric passport as well. As issuing times can currently take several weeks, families should check their documents for a Botswana holiday very early.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) about entry to Botswana

Can I enter Botswana using a national ID card?

That depends on whether Botswana is in the EU or the Schengen Area. For most long-haul destinations outside Europe, a standard passport is mandatory. A national ID card is then not sufficient.

What happens if my passport is not valid for long enough?

If the required validity period (for example, 6 months beyond the end of your trip) is not met, you will usually be refused boarding at the airport check-in desk. Airlines are required to check this strictly.

Do children need their own passport for Botswana?

Yes. Since 2024, older child passport arrangements are no longer sufficient for many countries outside the EU. In most cases, minors need a standard biometric passport (ePassport) for smooth entry.

Can I enter with an emergency or temporary passport?

This depends heavily on the destination country. While some countries accept a temporary passport together with a specific visa, countries such as the USA (under ESTA) or Dubai often reject it outright. Ideally, apply for a fast-track passport.

When will the airline refuse boarding?

Airline ground staff check your documents at the departure airport. If you cannot present a required visa (or your e-visa has not yet been approved), or if your passport does not meet the minimum validity of 6 months, boarding will be refused without exception.

Do I need return or onward travel tickets?

Most non-EU countries that allow tourists to enter visa-free or with a visa on arrival require proof of planned departure at border control. You should always be able to show your booked return ticket or onward ticket at passport control in Botswana (printed out or available offline on your phone).

Where is the safest place to apply for my visa?

For e-visas, always use only the official government websites of the destination country (often recognisable by endings such as .gov). The internet is full of dubious visa agencies that add a multiple of the actual fee as a "processing charge".
