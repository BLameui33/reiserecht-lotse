Problems with Airbnb because of AirCover Claim Rejected: your rights

Airbnb has revolutionised the way we travel. But when problems arise, many guests feel let down by the platform’s support. The automated chatbot rarely helps, and hosts often deny any responsibility. In particular, where AirCover Claim Rejected is concerned, acting quickly and keeping proper records is crucial.

What is AirCover for guests?

AirCover is Airbnb’s own buyer protection for guests. It is automatically included free of charge with every booking. AirCover applies, among other things, where the host cancels within 30 days before check-in, where you cannot access the accommodation, or where the listing was materially misdescribed. In theory, Airbnb promises you a refund or alternative accommodation. In practice, however, those who do not persist and make their demands in writing are often fobbed off with small vouchers.

The golden Airbnb rule: communicate only through the app!

Probably the most common mistake guests make is communicating with the host via WhatsApp or SMS. Airbnb has no access to those messages. If the host promises on WhatsApp to fix a problem but then fails to do so, you cannot prove that to Airbnb Support. Deal with complaints exclusively through the Airbnb message thread! That is your most important evidence for this claim letter.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) about Airbnb complaints

How long do I have to report a problem to Airbnb?

Airbnb has extremely strict deadlines: under the current policies, you must report a problem within 72 hours of discovering it (it used to be only 24 hours). If you only complain after check-out, the terms and conditions often mean you lose any entitlement to a refund through AirCover.

The host cancelled. Can I claim damages for a hotel?

If the host cancels at short notice, Airbnb will refund your money. In addition, AirCover helps with finding similar accommodation. If Airbnb cannot find an alternative and you have to book a more expensive hotel, you can try to claim the price difference as damages (additional costs). Airbnb does not always pay this voluntarily, but legally the host will often be liable for those losses.

Why is the letter addressed to Airbnb Ireland?

If you live in Europe, your user contract is with Airbnb Ireland UC. This is your legal contracting party for payments, fees and AirCover buyer protection. The UK or German marketing entities are often only responsible for marketing and may not be the correct legal addressee for claims.

Basis of the claim

Claims against Airbnb are based primarily on the platform’s own policies, supplemented by UK consumer contract law:

  • AirCover for guests & refund policies: The legal basis for most complaints made directly to the platform. These govern refunds where access is denied, bookings are cancelled and serious defects arise.
  • Host liability under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and general contract law: If Airbnb refuses a refund under AirCover, the host remains your direct contracting party for the accommodation agreement. Depending on the circumstances, you may be able to pursue a price reduction or damages under the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and general contract law.
