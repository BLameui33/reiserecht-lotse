Airport parking: how to avoid the parking charges trap

Anyone who likes to drive to the airport in their own car will know the problem: parking directly at the terminal often feels like daylight robbery. If you turn up without an online reservation and use the official car parks at major airports, you will often pay between £220 and well over £260 for 14 days. Parking can quickly become more expensive than the flight itself.

The solution is certified shuttle and valet parking in the immediate area around the airport. In our guide, you will find out what walk-up rates look like at your departure airport, how much money you can save by booking in advance, and what to look out for when choosing alternative parking operators.

The ultimate airport parking checklist

To make sure everything runs smoothly on the day you travel and you reach check-in without stress, work through these points in advance:

Checklist: airport parking

  • Compare prices & book: Use our calculator and book alternative parking no later than 2 weeks in advance.
  • Allow extra time for the shuttle: Allow around 30 to 45 minutes in addition to the airline’s check-in time to unload luggage and be taken to the terminal.
  • Keep flight numbers ready: When booking, make sure you provide your return flight number. That way, the shuttle bus will know when to collect you even if your flight is delayed.
  • Keep your booking confirmation safe: Print the booking confirmation including directions, or save it offline on your phone (including the driver’s emergency contact number).
  • Vehicle check (for valet): For your own protection, take 4 quick photos of your car on your phone when handing it over at the terminal (front, rear, sides).

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) about airport parking

Why is parking directly at the terminal so expensive?
Airport operators take advantage of passengers' desire for convenience. Car parks directly at the terminal are often aimed at business travellers whose company covers the cost, or at short-stay parkers (pick-ups and drop-offs). For ordinary holidaymakers, these walk-up rates are usually an unaffordable cost trap.
What is the difference between shuttle and valet parking?
With shuttle parking, you park your car yourself in a secure off-site car park and are taken to the terminal by a free minibus. With valet parking, you drive your car directly to the departures terminal and hand it over there to a member of staff, who parks it for you.
Is my car safe in alternative car parks?
Yes. Certified alternative car parks offered through major platforms (such as Parkos, ParkCare or Holiday Extras) are professionally secured. They are fenced, well lit, usually monitored by CCTV and often staffed around the clock.
When should I book my airport parking space?
Ideally, book your parking space as soon as your flight details are confirmed, but no later than 2 to 4 weeks before departure. Anyone who turns up at an airport car park without an online reservation will almost always pay the highest rate.
