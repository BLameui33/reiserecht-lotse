Fix my Trip

About us: your digital shield against travel-corporate bureaucracy

Fix my Trip is more than just another travel blog. We are your independent digital support platform. With our generators and checklists, we help you enforce your rights in cases of flight delays, travel complaints or unjustified charges actively, pragmatically and on equal footing – real help to help yourself.

The idea: act instead of getting frustrated

Anyone stranded at the airport after a flight cancellation, dealing with lost luggage or arriving in a dirty hotel room often has neither the time nor the patience for endless legal jargon. Airlines and tour operators take advantage of exactly that: they hide behind complex terms and conditions, blanket refusals and delaying template responses. Fix my Trip was born out of frustration with this tactic. We did not want to build mere information pages, but rather create practical tools you can actually use.

Whether you want to claim compensation under UK law, reclaim taxes or calculate a price reduction because of building noise: our generators translate complex legal rules into ready-to-use, legally robust wording aids.
Our promise (and our limit): We are not a law firm and we do not replace individual legal advice. But we give you the free tools you need so you are not fobbed off and can get the legal paperwork underway effectively.

Who is behind Fix my Trip?

Fix my Trip is run by a small, independent project team of experienced software developers and seasoned travellers. Rather than putting individuals in the spotlight, we prefer to let our work and our tools speak for themselves.

Because we do not work with large law firms or investors in the background, we rely on radical transparency: the calculations and documents from Fix my Trip are not based on mere opinions, but on the strict translation of applicable law into code. To do this, we systematically analyse legislation and current judgments and build that logic directly into our tools.

Our principles & way of working

To guarantee the reliability of our tools, we follow uncompromising principles:

1. Verifiable legal foundations

We do not guess and we do not make things up. Our generators are based exclusively on official sources such as UK Regulation 261 (UK261), the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or the established industry-standard ABTA guidelines for compensation.

2. Precise generator logic

Our PDF letters are not generated by AI without oversight. Every generator is programmed manually. The legal if-then logic (e.g. flight distance > 1,500 km = £350) is firmly built into the system and checked.

3. 100% independent & commission-free

We work strictly in travellers’ interests. We do not keep a 20% to 30% commission from successful compensation claims, as many large flight-claim portals do. You keep 100% of your money.

4. Uncompromising data protection

Your privacy is paramount to us. Our forms process your sensitive entries (such as flight numbers or addresses) securely. We do not store generated complaint documents on our servers.

Important note on use

The results of our checkers and generators are intended for non-binding initial assessment and procedural convenience. They do not replace individual legal advice from a qualified solicitor and do not guarantee the success of your claim against the travel provider. We review and update the legal position at regular intervals in light of new judgments.

How is Fix my Trip funded?

To keep our generators permanently free of charge and available without commission deductions, we cover our server and development costs through small, unobtrusive adverts on the website or through affiliate links to helpful travel tools (e.g. eSIM providers).

This funding never influences the editorial independence or the underlying logic of our legal generators.
