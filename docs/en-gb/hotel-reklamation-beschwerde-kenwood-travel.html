Holiday price reduction with Kenwood Travel Ltd: What you may be entitled to

Anyone booking a package holiday is not just paying for flights and accommodation, but for a legally protected overall package. If the reality on site differs significantly from the description in the brochure or booking confirmation, this may amount to a lack of conformity under the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018. In that case, UK package travel law may entitle you to a reduction in the holiday price. The key point is this: you are not asking for goodwill or a gesture of goodwill, but asserting a legally recognised claim against Kenwood Travel Ltd.

The most important step: reporting the problem on site

To avoid your complaint being rejected straight away by the organiser’s legal department, you must follow one basic rule: report the problem and ask for it to be put right. Inform Kenwood Travel Ltd’s local representative immediately (often reachable via hotline or during hotel office hours). Important: complaining only to the hotel reception is usually not enough in legal terms, because the hotel is merely a supplier and not your contractual organiser. Give the representative a reasonable opportunity to resolve the issue (for example by moving you to another room). Only if this remedy fails or is refused will you usually have a strong claim for money back.

What are the ABTA compensation guidelines?

A missing balcony, broken air conditioning or noise disturbance – how much is that worth? To help answer that question, many travellers and complaints handlers refer to industry-standard ABTA guidelines for compensation.

Although these are not binding law, they are widely used in the UK as a practical benchmark when assessing holiday complaints and price reductions. Our calculator uses these commonly accepted percentage ranges to place a realistic and enforceable claim in your PDF letter.

Compensation for loss of enjoyment

Was the holiday not just slightly defective, but a complete disaster? If the disruption was so serious that the enjoyment and value of the whole trip were substantially undermined, you may be entitled not only to a refund of part of the holiday price but also to additional compensation for loss of enjoyment. This can apply in extreme cases, such as serious food poisoning caused by the hotel buffet or complete overbooking without suitable replacement accommodation.

Frequently asked questions (FAQ) about complaining to Kenwood Travel Ltd

What is the best way to prove the problems?

Without solid evidence, Kenwood Travel Ltd will probably refuse payment. Document every problem immediately with photos or videos (including timestamps). Contact details for witnesses (other hotel guests) are also extremely helpful, as is a written record of the problems, ideally signed on site by the resort representative or tour representative.

What if I only complain once I am back home after the holiday?

That is a classic mistake. Under the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018, you should report the lack of conformity without undue delay to the organiser or its local representative while you are still on holiday. If you simply put up with the problem and only complain to the organiser after your return flight, you will usually weaken your position and may lose part or all of your claim for a price reduction.

How long do I have to bring a claim against the organiser?

Time limits can depend on the booking terms and the nature of the claim. In practice, package holiday claims are often subject to a limitation period of up to six years in England and Wales, but you should check your booking conditions and seek advice where needed. We still recommend submitting your complaint immediately after returning home while evidence and memories are still fresh.

Important notes on refunds from Kenwood Travel Ltd

Kenwood Travel is a specialist direct-sell luxury tour operator for holidays to the Caribbean, Middle East, and Asia. The Package Travel Regulations apply to bookings, meaning travellers generally have claims for a price reduction, remedy, or compensation in the event of travel defects like hotel problems or service deviations. Established industry compensation scales are frequently used for guidance. If you withdraw from the contract, cancellation fees may apply, depending on the timing and terms. Legal basis & recognised guidance

The complaints and price reduction claims in this generator are based on UK package travel law and widely recognised industry guidance:

  • Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018: These UK regulations set out the organiser’s obligations in detail. In particular, the rules on lack of conformity and travellers’ remedies form the legal basis for your refund claim.
    Official text on legislation.gov.uk
  • UK Consumer Rights Act 2015: Depending on the booking structure and the services provided, the Consumer Rights Act 2015 may also be relevant when assessing whether services were provided with reasonable care and skill and whether contractual descriptions were accurate.
    Official text on legislation.gov.uk
  • ABTA guidance on holiday complaints and compensation: Although not binding law, ABTA guidance is one of the most commonly used practical benchmarks in the UK for assessing the percentage value of compensation or price reductions for specific problems such as building noise, dirt or missing facilities.
    ABTA help and complaints guidance
  • Citizens Advice – package holiday problems: Citizens Advice provides practical consumer guidance on your rights if a package holiday goes wrong and on how to complain effectively.
    Citizens Advice guidance
