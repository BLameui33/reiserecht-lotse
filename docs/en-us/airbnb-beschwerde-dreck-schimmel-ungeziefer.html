Trouble with Airbnb regarding Mold, Dirt & Pests: Your Rights

Airbnb has revolutionized the way we travel. But when problems arise, many guests feel abandoned by the platform's support. The automated chatbot rarely helps, and hosts often deny any blame. Especially when it comes to Mold, Dirt & Pests, acting quickly and documenting everything is crucial.

What is AirCover for Guests?

AirCover is Airbnb's in-house buyer protection. It is automatically and free of charge included with every booking. AirCover applies, among other things, to host cancellations within 30 days before check-in, if you can't get into the accommodation, or if the listing was grossly misrepresented. Theoretically, Airbnb guarantees you a refund or alternative accommodation here. Practice shows, however: those who are not persistent and do not demand in writing are often fobbed off with small vouchers.

The Golden Airbnb Rule: Communication only via the App!

Probably the most common mistake made by guests: They communicate with the host via WhatsApp or SMS. Airbnb has no access to these messages. If the host promises improvement for a defect on WhatsApp but doesn't follow through, you can't prove it to Airbnb support. Resolve complaints exclusively via the Airbnb messaging system! This is your most important piece of evidence for this demand letter.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about Airbnb Complaints

How much time do I have to report an issue to Airbnb?

Airbnb has extremely strict deadlines: According to current policies, you must report a problem strictly within 72 hours of discovery (it used to be just 24 hours). According to the terms of service, those who only complain after check-out often lose any claim to a refund via AirCover.

The host canceled. Do I get damages for a hotel?

If the host cancels on short notice, you will get your money back from Airbnb. In addition, AirCover helps you find similar accommodation. If Airbnb finds no alternative and you have to book a more expensive hotel, you can try to claim the price difference as damages (additional costs). Airbnb does not always pay this voluntarily, but legally the host is often liable for damages.

Why is the letter addressed to Airbnb, Inc. in the US?

For bookings and users in the US market, you generally enter into your user agreement with Airbnb, Inc. This is your legal contracting party for payments, fees, and the AirCover buyer protection. Local or European branches are often legally the wrong addressee for claims here.

Basis of the Claim

Claims against Airbnb are based primarily on the platform's own policies and supplementary contract law:

  • AirCover for Guests & Rebooking and Refund Policy: The legal basis for most complaints directly against the platform. They regulate refunds for lack of access, cancellations, and serious defects.
  • Host Liability (Breach of Contract & Implied Warranty of Habitability): If Airbnb rejects a refund via AirCover, the host remains your direct contractual partner for the rental agreement. Under US law (depending on the respective state), you can sue them for damages.
