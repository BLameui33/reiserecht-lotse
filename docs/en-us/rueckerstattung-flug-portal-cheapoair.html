The OTA Trap: Why CheapOair Delays Your Refund

Booking a flight through portals like CheapOair might save you $10 or $20 upfront. But when a cancellation happens, this small discount turns into an expensive trap. Because your contract is with the middleman and not directly with the airline, the airline refunds the money to the agency's corporate credit card when a flight is canceled.

The "Ping-Pong" Game Between Airline and CheapOair

Did the airline cancel the flight and tell you they initiated the refund? Then your money is likely already sitting with CheapOair. If you ask them, you often receive automated responses like: "We are waiting for the airline's confirmation." This is frequently a deliberate delay tactic to hold onto the cash flow of thousands of customers. Legally, you have the right to a prompt disbursement of your funds once the agency receives them from the carrier.

Your Rights Under US DOT Regulations

Although you booked through a third party, U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) regulations explicitly state that passengers are entitled to a prompt and full refund when an airline cancels a flight, regardless of the reason.

  • Full Refund: For an airline-initiated cancellation, you are entitled to 100% of the ticket price back to your original form of payment.
  • Establish a Deadline: By sending a written demand letter (our PDF), you create a formal paper trail and set a strict deadline. This is the crucial "good faith effort" required before escalating to your bank.
  • No Vouchers: You do not have to accept travel vouchers or "credits" from CheapOair if the airline canceled the flight. You have a legal right to cash.

Frequently Asked Questions About Booking Portal Refunds

The airline says the agency has the money. CheapOair denies it. Who is lying?

In 95% of cases, the airline is telling the truth. Airlines process refunds back to the original form of payment. Since CheapOair bought the ticket on your behalf using their own corporate or virtual credit card, the money lands there. The portal then has to manually match this inbound refund to your customer account – and this is exactly where they delay the process.

What happens if CheapOair doesn't pay after the deadline?

Once the 14-day deadline expires, you have established proof that the merchant is refusing to refund you for services not rendered. You can then immediately file a chargeback dispute with your credit card company. Additionally, filing a complaint with the DOT Aviation Consumer Protection Division often forces the agency to act quickly to avoid federal fines.

Can CheapOair charge a processing fee for the cancellation?

If the airline canceled the flight, the agency is generally not allowed to deduct additional "processing" or "service" fees from the ticket refund. The DOT mandates a full refund of the ticket price, including taxes and fees.

Important Info Regarding CheapOair Refunds

CheapOair is an online travel agency (OTA) and not the operating airline. For ticket cancellations and refunds of taxes and fees, it is crucial to determine who issued the ticket and processed the payment. In practice, OTAs and airlines frequently point fingers at each other regarding refund delays. Legally, under US Department of Transportation (DOT) guidelines, if the airline issues a refund to CheapOair, the OTA must promptly pass it on to the consumer. Furthermore, OTAs are not permitted to charge unreasonable or deceptive processing fees for merely passing through a mandatory refund.
