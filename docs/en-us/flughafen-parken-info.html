Airport Parking: How to Avoid the Parking Fee Trap

Anyone who enjoys a relaxed drive to the airport in their own car knows the problem: parking directly at the terminal is often tantamount to financial highway robbery. If you drive spontaneously and without an online reservation into the official parking garages of major airports, you often pay between $250 and over $400 for 14 days. Parking quickly becomes more expensive than the actual flight itself.

The solution to this problem is certified shuttle and valet parking lots located in the immediate vicinity of the airport. In our guide, you will find out what the drive-up rates look like at your departure airport, how much money you can save with an advance reservation, and what you need to look out for with alternative parking lot operators.

The Ultimate Airport Parking Checklist

To ensure everything runs smoothly on your day of departure and you reach check-in without stress, work through these points in advance:

Checklist: Airport Parking

  • Compare prices & book: Use our calculator and book alternative parking lots at least 2 weeks in advance.
  • Plan a time buffer for the shuttle: Plan an additional 30 to 45 minutes on top of the airline's check-in time to unload your luggage and be driven to the terminal.
  • Have flight numbers ready: Be sure to provide your return flight number when booking. This way, the shuttle bus knows when to pick you up, even if your flight is delayed.
  • Save your booking confirmation: Print out the booking confirmation including directions, or save it offline on your phone (including the driver's emergency number).
  • Vehicle check (for Valet): For your own security, take 4 quick photos of your car on your phone (front, back, sides) when handing over the vehicle at the terminal.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) about Airport Parking

Why is parking right at the terminal so expensive?
Airport operators take advantage of passenger convenience. The parking garages directly at the terminal are often aimed at business travelers whose companies cover the costs, or at short-term parkers (people picking up passengers). For regular vacationers, these drive-up rates are usually an unaffordable cost trap.
What is the difference between shuttle and valet parking?
With shuttle parking, you park your car yourself in a secure off-site parking lot and are taken to the terminal in a free minibus. With valet parking, you drive your car directly to the departure terminal and hand it over to a staff member who parks it for you.
Is my car safe in alternative parking lots?
Yes. Certified alternative parking lots booked through major portals (such as SpotHero, Way.com, or The Parking Spot) are professionally secured. They are fenced, well-lit, mostly video-monitored, and often staffed around the clock.
When should I book my airport parking space?
Ideally, you should book your parking space as soon as your flight details are fixed, but no later than 2 to 4 weeks before departure. Anyone who drives into the airport parking garage spontaneously without an online reservation invariably pays the highest rate.
