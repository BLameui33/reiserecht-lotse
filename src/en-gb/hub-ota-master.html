Steps to counter OTA delay tactics

  • Obtain airline confirmation: Ask the airline to confirm in writing (email/chat) that the refund has already been paid to the intermediary.
  • No vouchers: Do not accept "wallet credits" or vouchers from the portal. Demand cash back to your account.
  • Set a deadline: Use our tool to generate a 14-day deadline letter and send it to the portal by email.
  • Prepare a chargeback: Keep records of all contact attempts. If the deadline expires, contact your bank about a credit card chargeback.
  • Costs of default: Inform the portal that, after the deadline expires, it may also be liable for additional recovery costs under the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and general UK contract law principles.

FAQ: help with the OTA trap

Why should I not sue the airline directly?
If the airline has already refunded the money to the intermediary, it has fulfilled its obligation. Your claim is then against the portal that is holding your money.
How do I find the correct address for the portal?
Many portals are based abroad (Spain, the Czech Republic, etc.). In our generators, the official addresses of the parent companies (such as Vacaciones eDreams or Kiwi.com s.r.o.) are already stored for you.
What is the benefit of alternative dispute resolution?
ADR bodies such as AviationADR can help free of charge with disputes in some cases. However, many OTAs deal with complaints and ADR referrals extremely slowly. A properly drafted deadline letter is always the first and most important step in putting the portal in default.
